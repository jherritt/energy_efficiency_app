Your body runs on a battery. Amperly shows you the charge.

Every morning you wake up with a certain amount of energy. Amperly reads your sleep from Apple Health and turns it into a glowing battery: a good night charges you up, a short or restless one leaves you running low. Then, as you move through your day, life draws that battery down. You can see it happen in real time.

One number tells you how well you are doing: your efficiency score, out of 100. Efficiency is not about burning the most calories. It rewards spending the energy you have on the things that count, so a calm, well-paced day can score higher than a frantic one.

CHARGED BY SLEEP
Your morning battery comes straight from last night's sleep, measured against your own target and the quality of your rest. Sleep well and you start the day full.

DRAINED BY YOUR DAY
Activity and the simple passage of the day draw the battery down. Watch it deplete, and notice when you are running low before you feel it.

POINTS FOR WHAT MATTERS
Earn points for the habits that keep your energy healthy:
- Move, Exercise, and Stand goals
- An on-time bedtime and an on-time wake
- Staying hydrated
The points ledger shows what you have earned today and what is still within reach.

ONE EFFICIENCY SCORE
A single hero number out of 100 that captures how well you spent your energy against your goals. Simple to read, hard to fake.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Amperly collects nothing. It reads Apple Health read-only, with your permission, computes everything on your device, and stores or transmits nothing. No account. No sign-in. No analytics, no ads, no trackers. Your health data is never used for advertising and never leaves your phone.

WIDGETS EVERYWHERE
Keep your battery and efficiency score in view on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen, and in StandBy. The widgets use the same on-device calculation as the app, so they always agree.

DESIGNED TO BE CALM
A dark-first electric theme with full light-mode support, clean typography, and Dynamic Type throughout. Premium, quiet, and easy to read at a glance.

FREE
Amperly is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Charge up. Spend well. See your day as a battery.
