# Amperly Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-06-28**

Amperly is built on a simple promise: your health data is yours, and it never leaves your device. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what that means.

## The short version

Amperly collects **no data**. It does not require an account. It reads information from Apple Health on your device, with your permission, computes your daily battery, points, and efficiency score entirely on your device, and then displays the result. Nothing is stored by us, transmitted off your device, sold, or shared with anyone. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

## What Amperly reads

When you grant permission, Amperly reads the following categories from Apple Health on a **read-only** basis. It cannot and does not write anything back to Apple Health.

- Sleep (time asleep, in bed, and sleep stages)
- Activity (active and resting energy, steps, exercise minutes, stand hours, and your Move, Exercise, and Stand goals)
- Heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV)
- Hydration (water intake)
- Time in daylight
- Caffeine (used only to note timing for the day's view)

You choose which categories to allow. If you decline a category, Amperly simply shows `--` where that information would appear, and continues to work with whatever you have shared.

## How your data is used

All processing happens **on your device, in real time**. Amperly takes the values it reads from Apple Health, calculates your battery level, points, and efficiency score, and shows them to you. These calculations happen in memory and are discarded when you leave the relevant screen. The Amperly widgets use the same on-device calculation to display your battery and score on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and in StandBy.

Amperly does not:

- Store your health data in any database, file, or cache that we can read.
- Send your health data, or any other personal data, off your device.
- Use your health data for advertising. HealthKit data is never used for advertising or marketing.
- Sell or share your data with any third party.
- Maintain user accounts, profiles, or identifiers.
- Include analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs).

Because there is no account and no server, there is no record of you for us to lose, leak, or hand over.

## Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Your health data remains where it always was: in Apple Health, under your control. Amperly's view of it exists only for as long as a screen or widget is showing it.

## How to revoke access

You can withdraw Amperly's access to any or all Health categories at any time:

1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone.
2. Tap **Privacy and Security**, then **Health**.
3. Tap **Amperly**.
4. Turn off any categories you no longer wish to share, or turn them all off.

You can also adjust permissions from within the Health app under **Sharing**. Revoking access takes effect immediately; Amperly will show `--` for anything it can no longer read.

## Children's privacy

Amperly is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because Amperly collects no data at all and has no account system, no personal information from any user, including children, is gathered, stored, or transmitted. Health data is read only on the device and used only to display on-device results.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the revised version at the same location. Because Amperly's design is built around collecting nothing, we do not anticipate changes that would weaken these protections.
