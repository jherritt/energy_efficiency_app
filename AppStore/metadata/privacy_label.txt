App Privacy Nutrition Label Summary

Data Not Collected
The developer does not collect any data from this app.

Details

Amperly has no account system and no server. It reads Apple Health data on a read-only basis, with the user's explicit permission, and processes everything on the device.

Health data read (read-only, with permission):
- Sleep (duration, time in bed, sleep stages)
- Activity (active and resting energy, steps, exercise minutes, stand hours, and Move, Exercise, and Stand goals)
- Heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV)
- Hydration (water intake)
- Time in daylight
- Caffeine (timing only)

How the data is handled:
- All computation happens on device, in real time.
- No health data is stored to disk, cached for us to read, or written back to Apple Health.
- No health data is transmitted off the device.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.
- Health data is never used for tracking or advertising.
- There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in the app.

Because nothing is collected, stored, or transmitted, the correct App Privacy answer for every category is Data Not Collected. If a category permission is denied, the app displays "--" and continues to function with whatever the user has shared.
