Version 1.0

The first release of Amperly.

- See your day as a glowing battery, charged by last night's sleep and drained as you go.
- Earn points for Move, Exercise, and Stand goals, on-time bedtime and wake, and hydration.
- Read one hero efficiency score out of 100 that shows how well you spent your energy.
- Keep your battery and efficiency score on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and in StandBy with widgets.
- Private by design: read-only Apple Health access, all computation on device, nothing stored or transmitted, no account.

Thank you for trying Amperly. We would love your feedback.
