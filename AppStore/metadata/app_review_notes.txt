APP REVIEW NOTES - Amperly

WHAT THE APP DOES
Amperly visualizes the user's daily energy as a battery that is charged by sleep and drained across the waking day, awards points for healthy behaviors (Apple Move/Exercise/Stand, on-time sleep/wake, hydration), and shows a single 0 to 100 efficiency score for how well the user converts spent energy into completed points. It also provides Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED
There is no sign-in, registration, or login of any kind. The app is fully usable immediately after granting Apple Health access. No demo account is needed.

HEALTH DATA USAGE (read-only, on-device)
- All data is read from Apple HealthKit with the user's permission and is processed entirely on the device.
- The app requests READ access only. It never writes to HealthKit and requests no share/update scopes.
- Nothing is stored, transmitted, sold, or shared. There is no server, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking. HealthKit data is never used for advertising or marketing.
- Types read: sleep analysis, active/basal energy, exercise minutes, stand hours, step count, dietary water, heart rate variability (SDNN), resting heart rate, dietary caffeine, time in daylight, workouts, and the activity-summary ring goals. Each is used only to compute the on-screen battery, points, and efficiency numbers.
- The App Privacy answer is "Data Not Collected."

HOW TO TEST
1. Launch the app and tap "Connect Apple Health," then grant access to the requested categories.
2. If the test device or simulator has little or no Health data, the battery and efficiency will show "--" placeholders (by design, the app never fabricates numbers). To see populated values, either test on a device that already has Apple Watch sleep/activity data, or add a few sample entries in the Health app (for example a Sleep session and some Steps/Active Energy and Water) and reopen Amperly.
3. Widgets: add the Amperly widgets to the Home Screen and Lock Screen. They read Health live and render the same battery and efficiency values, or "--" when access is not granted.

NOTIFICATIONS
The app sends no remote/push notifications. It can schedule a single, local, opt-in "low energy efficiency" reminder only if the user explicitly enables it in Settings. It is off by default.
